Changes: preserve the encoder and slice the final CTC classifier to Latin
letters, digits, punctuation, currency symbols, space and blank. Output
probabilities are therefore not directly comparable to the full model's.
The filtered dictionary is derived from upstream ONNX character metadata.
No upstream runtime or package code is included. Reproduce the assets with
repository tool tools/prepare-comic-recognizer.py.
